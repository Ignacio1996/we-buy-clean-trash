⚖️ Depot Worker — The Sorter

At the depot. Opens bags, separates materials, weighs each commodity separately, enters data. Replaces paper + Excel process.
Receive delivery → Scan bag QR → Open & sort → Weigh each material → Submit & next
NOTE Process Bag is wired end-to-end: POST /api/process-bag runs calculatePoints from src/lib/logic, then in one Admin SDK transaction writes bagProcessing + transactions ledger entry, increments users.pointsBalance, updates inventory, and fires the SMS stub.
FRIDAY MEETING · APR 27 The process form now only renders material rows the depot is configured to accept (set per-depot from admin Zones & Depots). The server rejects weights for unaccepted materials. A 🔥 Active campaign banner appears above contamination when global multipliers are running, and the points preview / submit reflect the multiplier. The applied campaigns are snapshotted onto each bagProcessing doc for audit. The depot Inventory page now lists any material the depot has actually processed (including custom ones like compost / textile / electronics), not just the canonical 7.
STICKER SECTIONS · MAY 25 Depot worker surface migrated to Sticker Sections. The four screens above (Queue / Scan / Process bag / Inventory) replace the legacy dark UI. Same primitives as the resident app — .ss-block color sections, .ss-btn.green for primary action, .ss-bar for stock + queue progress, .ss-viewfinder for the QR scan view — retuned for a depot-floor context (high-contrast, fat tap targets, glove-friendly buttons).

How does a resident earn points?
Residents fill QR-tagged bags and set them out on pickup day. At the depot each bag is weighed per commodity; points are weight × market price × customer %, doubled if separated, reduced for contamination — then ×100 (100 pts = $1). The signup bonus is 10 pts.

✅ DECISIONS Resolved decisions (locked-in)
QR vs barcode, sticker model, 7 categories, weighing process, voice prompts, AI scope, bags reorder, recycling education, rewards model, invoicing.
✅ QR vs barcode → Using QR codes. Each QR has a printed number next to it as fallback.

✅ Rewards model → Points linked to gift cards (Amazon, Walmart). 1,000 pts = $10. Pilot: ~$1,000 in gift cards for ~100 users in local area.
