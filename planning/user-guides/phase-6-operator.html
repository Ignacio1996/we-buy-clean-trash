Phase 6 · Running an Operator Day

What the driver does from the moment they open the app in the morning to the moment they return to the depot. All scenarios in this guide are run as Operator Aguirre on a phone browser, with Tia and Aguirre on the side to set things up and verify.

What you're verifying

  • Aguirre sees today's route when Tia has assigned it to him.
  • He can start the route, work through stops, and close it out.
  • Each stop's QR scan matches the expected resident — scanning the wrong bag is rejected.
  • "Skip", "Not out", and "Issue" all behave differently.
  • Delivering a sheet of new bags to a resident registers the 10 bags in the resident's account.
  • After the route is closed, the resident's bag shows up in the depot's incoming list (will be checked when Phase 7 lands).
Setup before you start. Make sure Tia has already built today's route using the Phase 5 guide. The route must be assigned to Aguirre and include at least one pickup stop (Aguirre) and one delivery stop (Goode or Aguirre, whichever has a pending bag order).
Scenario 1 — Seeing today's route

Operator Signed in as Operator Aguirre on a phone.

  1. Open the site on the phone. Sign in as Aguirre.
  2. Land on the operator home screen.
Expected: The screen shows a summary row at the top (Stops done / total, Bags, Deliveries), a "Ready to roll?" card asking Aguirre to start the route, and a preview of the first stop.

The operator home screen loads without needing admin menus

Aguirre sees Aguirre's and Goode's addresses — not anyone else's

If no route is scheduled, the screen says "No active route" (not an error)

Scenario 2 — Start route and tap "Driver on the way"

Operator Signed in as Aguirre.

  1. Tap ▶ Start route.
  2. Once the first stop's card is showing, tap 📲 Text resident: driver on the way.
Expected: The "Ready to roll?" card disappears and the current stop card becomes interactive. The "on the way" button fires instantly and shows a confirmation ("Text sent" or similar).

During the pilot the SMS isn't actually sent — it's simulated. You don't need to check Aguirre's phone. The test is just that the button responds.

Start Route transitions the screen cleanly

"On the way" button shows a confirmation toast

Scenario 3 — Complete a pickup

Operator Signed in as Aguirre.

For this test, the "resident" can just be a printed QR on a table — you don't have to drive anywhere.

  1. On the current stop (Aguirre's house), tap ✓ Scan & complete.
  2. Allow camera access. Point the camera at Aguirre's pre-scanned bag QR sticker.
  3. If the camera is being stubborn, tap Enter code manually and type the printed bag number (e.g. BAG-4237-03).
  4. Leave Bag sealed properly on (green).
  5. Leave Visible contamination off (grey).
  6. Take a quick photo (any photo is fine — it's the "I was here" record).
  7. Tap Confirm pickup.
Expected: A checkmark flashes. Aguirre is returned to today's route, which now shows "1 of 2 stops done" (or similar) and moves the current-stop card to the next address.

The wrong QR is rejected — try scanning a random QR code (from anywhere) and it should refuse

The pickup counter increments on the home screen

If Aguirre had 3 bags, Aguirre has to repeat this 3 times — one per bag

Scenario 4 — Skip vs Not Out vs Issue

Operator Signed in as Aguirre.

For this one, it's easiest to set up a third test resident you can abuse. If you don't have one, just reuse Goode — do the Skip test first, then come back and Not-out him.

Skip

  1. Tap ⏭ Skip on Goode's stop.
  2. Continue with the other stops.
  3. Reload the page.
Expected: After reload, Goode's stop reappears at the end of the list. Skipping is session-only — useful for "I'll come back to this one later today."

Not out

  1. Tap 📦 Not out on Goode's stop.
Expected: The stop is marked missed permanently. It does not come back on reload. Aguirre moves on.

Issue

  1. Set up a fourth test stop (or use any stop you haven't completed).
  2. Tap ⚠ Issue.
  3. Pick Contaminated, type a short note like "Bag full of food waste."
  4. Submit.
Expected: The stop is flagged as having an issue with Aguirre's note. Tia can see the note later in the route summary / admin view.

Skip is session-only

Not out persists across reload

Issue records the note

Scenario 5 — Deliver a fresh sticker sheet

Operator Signed in as Aguirre.

Goode ordered bags in Phase 5. Today Aguirre is delivering them. In real life Aguirre would carry printed sticker sheets of 10 QRs each; for testing, the dev team will give you one printed sheet per test order, or you can use a dummy QR sheet.

  1. At Goode's stop, look for the green Deliver bags + stickers sub-card.
  2. Tap it.
  3. Scan the first sticker on a fresh sheet — the one with the lowest printed number (ending in -01).
Expected: The app claims all 10 stickers for Goode at once. Goode's account now has 10 unused bag stickers available. If you sign in as Goode and go to Scan, he should be able to scan any of those 10 bags.

Scanning the first sticker succeeds

Scanning a middle sticker (e.g. -05) is rejected with a helpful message

Goode's unused-bag count goes up by 10

Scenario 6 — End of day

Operator Signed in as Aguirre.

  1. Work through the rest of the stops (complete, not out, or issue — each stop needs some outcome).
  2. Once everything has an outcome, the home screen replaces the current-stop card with All stops handled ✓.
  3. Tap End-of-route summary.
  4. Read the numbers — Stops visited, Bags picked up, Missed, Issues, Deliveries fulfilled.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and tap Deliver to depot.
Expected: The route flips to completed. It disappears from Aguirre's home (tomorrow's route will replace it when built). Tia can see the completed route with all its outcomes in the admin routes list.

Summary numbers match what Aguirre actually did

"Deliver to depot" is a one-way confirmation

After closing, Aguirre cannot edit stops

Once closed, it's closed. Deliberately try to re-open the route after tapping Deliver to depot. Aguirre should not be able to. If he can, that's a bug.

Quick troubleshooting

What you see Likely cause What to try
"No active route" Tia didn't assign a route to Aguirre for today, or assigned it for the wrong date. Have Tia check /admin/routes.
Camera stays black Browser blocked camera access. Reload, grant permission, or use manual code entry.
Scan keeps saying "wrong bag" The QR you're scanning isn't assigned to this address. Double-check you're at the right stop. Try manual entry. If you're sure, flag as an Issue.
Start route button does nothing Route is already started on another device. Reload — the current stop card should already be showing.

What's not testable yet

Aguirre finishing a route is meant to hand the pickups over to a depot worker for weighing and points awarding. That depot flow is Phase 7, which isn't built yet — so after "Deliver to depot," nothing further happens for the resident. Aguirre won't see points for her pickup until Phase 7 is in. That's expected for this build.
