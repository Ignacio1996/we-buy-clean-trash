Phase 4 · Admin Dashboard

Sarah's back office: invites, zones, depots, material pricing, and the gift card queue. All scenarios in this guide are run as Sarah Chen.

What you're verifying

  • The overview dashboard shows real numbers, not placeholders.
  • Sarah can send an operator invitation and it arrives (or simulates arriving).
  • Zones and depots can be created, edited, and deleted.
  • Material prices save and the resident calculator immediately reflects the change.
  • Gift card requests from residents appear in the redemption queue.

Scenario 5 — Yellow-sheet pricing editor

Admin Signed in as Sarah Chen.

  1. In the sidebar, click Pricing.
  2. You'll see 7 rows — one per material (aluminum, tin/steel, cardboard, paper, PET, HDPE, mixed plastic).
  3. Each row has a market price ($/lb) and a customer payout %.
  4. Change aluminum's market price to $1.00/lb. Save.
  5. In a second browser, open Maria's account and go to her Calculator.
  6. Enter 1 lb of aluminum.
Expected: With a 30% payout, 1 lb should yield 3,000 points (= $0.30). Double-check by flipping on Separated — points should roughly double to 6,000.

Price change saves and persists on reload

Resident calculator reflects the new price within a few seconds

A history of previous prices is visible (snapshot each time you save)

Scenario 6 — Redemption queue

Admin Signed in as Sarah Chen.

Prerequisite: Maria requested a gift card in Phase 3, Scenario 7. If you haven't done that yet, do it now.

  1. In the sidebar, click Redemptions.
  2. Find Maria's pending Amazon gift card request.
  3. Pretend you've emailed her the gift card code out of band (during the pilot we do this manually).
  4. Click Mark fulfilled.
Expected: The request moves from Pending to Fulfilled. In Maria's own Rewards history, the entry changes status to Fulfilled too.

Status flips on both sides (admin queue and Maria's history)

Points are not refunded when fulfilling (they were already deducted)

Admin sidebar at a glance

Menu item What Sarah does there
Overview KPI dashboard. Read-only.
Users & Invites List residents, send invitations to employees.

Pricing Edit the 7 material prices and payout percentages.
Redemptions Approve gift card requests one at a time.
Routes Build the daily pickup route and assign it to an operator (covered in the Routes guide — Phase 5).
