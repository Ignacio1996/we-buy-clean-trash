We Buy Clean Trash

The full lifecycle — from doorstep to recycling mill

Status: Pilot app is built and live. All 10 steps below are implemented end-to-end — resident, operator, depot worker, depot manager, and admin roles all have working apps. A few integrations are running on stubs for the pilot (flagged below) and will be swapped for real vendors before scale.
📸

Try Before You Sign Up

Before creating an account, anyone can scan a few recyclables and see how much they'd earn. Scan a water bottle, a Coke can — the app shows points and dollar value instantly. Lowest time-to-value first screen.

✅ BUILT · Scan UI + item flow live ⚠️ Gemini 2.5 Flash not yet integrated — results mocked for pilot

Sign Up & Order Bags

Customer creates an account and orders recycling bags. Bags come with a sheet of QR code stickers — each sticker has a unique QR code plus a printed number as backup. Customer sticks one label on each bag before filling it.

💰 Revenue: bag sales (primary) ✅ BUILT ⚠️ Stripe stubbed — mock checkout for pilot
♻️

Fill & Separate

Two bag types: Cardboard & Paper in one bag, Commingled (plastic, aluminum, tin, glass) in another. Separated earns 2x points. A quick recycling guide in the app shows good vs. bad examples — empty rinsed bottles vs. half-full ones, clean boxes vs. greasy pizza boxes.

✅ BUILT · In-app recycling guide live

Scan QR & Schedule

Customer scans the QR sticker on the bag in the app, selects the bag type, takes a photo, and places it at the door. The app confirms their next scheduled pickup. If the QR is damaged, they type the printed number instead.

✅ BUILT · jsQR scan + manual fallback + photo upload
🔔

Text Notifications

Resident gets a text 30 minutes before pickup: "Put your bags out!" Then a real-time alert when the driver is on the way: "Your trashman is heading to you now." Extra time to grab one more bag if needed.

✅ BUILT · Trigger logic + message log live ⚠️ Twilio stubbed — messages log to DB for pilot

Operator Picks Up

Driver follows the app route, stop by stop. At each address: one tap on the big "Complete" button to confirm pickup. Skip or flag issues with secondary buttons. Fast — no typing, no stopping. QR scan + manual number entry for detailed logging. Also delivers new bags and QR sticker sheets to residents who reordered.

✅ BUILT · Operator app + route stepper live ⚠️ Google Maps Routes API not yet integrated — stop order manual for pilot
⚖️

Depot: Open, Sort & Weigh

At the depot, worker scans the bag QR. Opens it on a table and separates materials. Each commodity is weighed individually — PET plastic, aluminum, tin, cardboard, paper — and entered into the app. Replaces the old paper-and-Excel process. No AI here yet; keep it simple and functional.

✅ BUILT · 7-commodity weigh-in + contamination severity + live inventory

⭐

Points Awarded

Points are auto-calculated based on each material's weight and current market price. The customer receives a text notification with their points earned. Contaminated or poorly recycled bags earn up to 90% less.

✅ BUILT · Atomic ledger writes + balance updates on every bag
🎁

Redeem Gift Cards

1,000 points = $10 gift card. Redeem for Amazon, Walmart, or local store gift cards. Can also apply credit toward their trash bill or donate to a nonprofit. Points are linked to real rewards from day one — no abstract numbers.

✅ BUILT · Redemption requests + admin fulfillment queue ⚠️ Cards sent manually by admin during pilot; Pay Trash Bill + Donate are disabled

Sell to Mills

Sorted materials are palletized and sold to domestic and international recycling mills at market rates (the "yellow sheet"). 7 commodity categories tracked: aluminum, tin, cardboard, paper, PET, HDPE, and mixed plastic.

💰 Revenue: ~70% margin on sales ✅ BUILT · Mill shipments + admin yellow-sheet price editor

Compost Clubhouse Integration

Tia's Compost Clubhouse — commercial food-scrap collection serving 19 active sites in Columbus — is being migrated off Linktree + Google Sheets into the same WBCT platform. Same code, same admin, same data model, same login. Different material, different measurement, different reward. Phases A + B have shipped; Phase C (city reports) is the last piece.

✅ PHASE A · SHIPPED

Foundation primitives

  • Materials can be cash (pays points) or diversion-only (records weight, no points). Compost, textiles, electronics, donations all use diversion-only.
  • Materials declare a measurement mode: bag-weight (existing) or bin-fullness (new). Bin-fullness lookup table seeded from Tia's spreadsheet.
  • Per-depot accepted-materials chip list iterates the live materials collection — custom materials appear and toggle per depot.
  • Depot worker can flag a bag commingled at processing — weight recorded for diversion, points forced to 0.
  • Two new seeded materials: commingled and food_scrap.
Kirk benefits too: add/remove materials anytime, track diverted-only items (textiles, electronics donations), commingled bags handled exactly per his Apr 27 ask.
✅ PHASE B · SHIPPED

Driver-side flow + commercial accounts

  • Reusable bin containers — admin mints BIN-NNNN QR codes per site; bins stay active across pickups instead of retiring.
  • Operator compost route at /operator/compost — today's sites filtered by zone, large-touch fullness pickers per bin, total weight derived live from the bin-weight table.
  • Commercial accounts at /admin/commercial-accounts — admin-onboarded with business name, address, contact, default bin size, pickups/week, collection days, affiliation tag, driver notes. Soft-delete preserves history.
  • Bin pickup ledger — /api/process-bin-pickup writes a binPickups doc + commercial inventory namespace per zone × material.
  • Resident UI gates — commercial-site portals see only Home + Profile; calculator / order-bags / scan-bag bounce back. Home shows 30-day diversion total + recent pickups instead of points.
Tia's drivers can stop using Linktree. Full notes in planning/Compost Integration - Phase B.md.
🔜 PHASE C · REPORTING

City compliance reports + roles

  • Diversion report export — admin filters by zone + date range → CSV/PDF in Dominique's Columbus monthly format. Replaces her manual Sheet pull.
  • Program manager role for Dominique — zone-scoped read+admin on commercial accounts, pickups, reports.
  • City viewer role for Ari (City of Columbus) — read-only on diversion reports for the CoC affiliation.
  • Cars-equivalent + CO2eq impact stats on commercial-account dashboards (ReFED constants: 1 lb = 0.00245 t CO2eq).
  • Optional: compost-voucher redemption queue once the Columbus facility is producing. Skip until then.
Unblocks the city contract reporting Tia owes monthly.
WHAT CHANGES FOR EXISTING WBCT
Behavior for residents and existing operators: nothing changes. All seven cash commodities still pay points exactly the same way; legacy bags default to containerType: 'bag' and users to accountType: 'resident'. The compost track is additive — admin gains material CRUD, per-depot acceptance, and the diversion-only mode; depot gains the commingled toggle; operators gain the compost route alongside their residential route. Full notes in planning/Compost Integration - Phase A.md and Phase B.md.
