Phase 1 · Sign-up & Sign-in

How new residents join, and how employees accept invitations. Test this phase first — every later phase assumes Aguirre, Goode, Tia, and Aguirre all have working accounts.

What you're verifying

  • A resident can sign up from the public homepage with their email or a Google account.
  • A signed-up resident lands on the resident dashboard — not the admin or operator area.
  • An admin can sign in and lands in the admin dashboard.
  • An employee (operator) cannot sign up directly — they need an invite link.
  • Signing out returns everyone to the public homepage.
Scenario 1 — Resident signs up with email

Resident Signed in as: Resident Aguirre · resident.aguirre@example.com

  1. Open the public homepage in a fresh private/incognito window.
  2. Click Sign up.
  3. Enter Aguirre's name, her email, and a password you'll remember. Use a real street address in the pilot zone.
  4. Submit the form.
Expected: Aguirre lands on the resident home screen. It greets her by name and shows 10,000 points as a signup bonus. A short 3-step onboarding card ("Order bags → Schedule → Earn") appears.

Scenario 4 — Operator cannot self-sign-up

Tester You, in a new private window. No one signed in yet.

  1. Open the homepage.
  2. Click Sign up.
  3. Look at the sign-up form. It only creates resident accounts — there's no role picker, no "I'm an operator" option.
Expected: There's no way to self-select the operator, depot, or admin role. The only path to those roles is an emailed invitation from an admin. You'll test that in Scenario 5 after Tia sends Aguirre an invite in the Admin Dashboard guide.

No role selector anywhere on sign-up

Scenario 5 — Operator accepts an invite

Operator Will be signed in as: Operator Aguirre · operator.aguirre@wbct-pilot.test

Prerequisite: Tia has already invited Aguirre from the admin dashboard. If you haven't done that yet, do the Admin Dashboard guide first and come back here.

  1. Open the invite email Tia sent to Aguirre. (During the pilot the email is simulated — the dev team will hand you a direct link.)
  2. Click Accept invitation.
  3. Set Aguirre's password when prompted.
  4. Submit.
Expected: Aguirre lands on the operator home screen (Today's Route). He sees either "No active route" or, if Tia already built one for him today, a route card.

Aguirre lands on the operator screen, not resident

The same invite link, if clicked again, says "already used"

Signing out

From any signed-in screen, open the top-right menu and pick Sign out. You should return to the public homepage. If you reload the page and still see the logged-in view, that's a bug.

Gotcha — wrong area after sign-in. If a resident signs in and somehow lands on the admin page, or vice versa, that's a role-routing bug. Write it down with the exact email used.

Quick troubleshooting

What you see Probably because What to do
"This address is outside our service area" Your test address isn't in the configured pilot zone. Ask the dev team to add the zip code to the zone list.
Google sign-in popup closes with no result Browser popup blocker or a third-party cookie setting. Allow popups for the site and retry.
Invite link says "invalid or expired" The invite was already accepted, or Tia revoked it. Ask Tia to send a fresh one.
