Phase 7 · Running a Depot Day

What the depot worker does from the moment a route arrives to the moment the resident's points balance ticks up. All scenarios are run as Depot Goode on a tablet or laptop, with Tia, Aguirre, and Aguirre on the side to set things up and verify.

⚠️ What's stubbed in this flow The depot flow itself is real — points, ledger, and inventory all update for real. The only touches that are stubbed:
  • Weight entry is manual. No Bluetooth or USB scale integration — weigh on your scale, type the number in.
  • "Bag processed" SMS to the resident is logged to the server console, not sent by text.

What you're verifying (happy path, ~15 min)

  • Goode gets invited and lands on her depot's Incoming screen.
  • Aguirre's completed routes show up under "Incoming deliveries."
  • Scanning a bag QR — or typing the printed number — jumps to the process form.
  • Submitting the form awards points to Aguirre, appends a transaction ledger entry, marks the bag processed, and adds weight to the depot inventory — all in one atomic write.

Scenarios 1–4 below cover the happy path. Everything after is optional advanced testing — edge cases most testers can skip.

📋 Before you start

Device: 💻 Laptop or tablet (Goode) + 📱 Phone (Aguirre, for a prior pickup) + 💻 Laptop on the side (Tia, to verify).

Dependencies — tick each before running Scenario 1:

  • Phase 4 — Yellow Sheet prices set for all 7 materials.
  • Phase 4 — a Depot exists that Tia can link Goode to.
  • Phase 5 — a route built for Aguirre that includes a pickup for Aguirre.
  • Phase 6 · Scenario 6 — Aguirre completed the route and tapped Deliver to depot.

If any of those are missing, Goode's Incoming screen will be empty.

Scenario 1 — Invite the depot worker

Admin Signed in as Tia on a laptop.

  1. Open /admin/invites.
  2. Click New invite.
  3. Enter Goode's email, pick role Depot worker.
  4. A Depot dropdown appears once the role is set. Pick the pilot depot (the one Tia created in Phase 4 — e.g. "Depot #01 — Ohio West").
  5. Send the invite.
Expected: An invite link is generated. Tia copies it and sends it to Goode. When Goode opens the link, sets a password, and lands in the app, her user record now has both the depot_worker role and the depotId pointing at the pilot depot.

Verify:

The Depot dropdown only appears for depot-worker / depot-manager invites

Goode lands at /depot/incoming after accepting

The depot name in the header matches the depot Tia picked

Scenario 2 — Seeing incoming deliveries

Depot Signed in as Goode on a tablet.

For this scenario you need a route Aguirre has already closed out (Phase 6, Scenario 6). If he hasn't, jump back and finish that first.

  1. Open the app and sign in as Goode.
  2. Land on the depot home, which redirects to /depot/incoming.
Expected: A single card for Aguirre's route, labelled NEW. The row shows Aguirre's name, the number of bags he actually picked up, and the arrival time (whenever he tapped Deliver to depot).

Verify:

Missed / issue bags are not counted in the bag total

Routes from a zone that isn't this depot's don't appear

If nothing has arrived yet, the screen shows a friendly empty-state, not an error

Scenario 3 — Scan a bag into the process form

Depot Signed in as Goode.

  1. From Incoming, tap Aguirre's route row.
  2. The route-detail screen lists every picked-up bag with a PENDING badge.
  3. Tap 📷 Scan a bag.
  4. Allow camera access. Point the camera at one of Aguirre's bag QR stickers.
  5. If the camera is being stubborn, tap the Or type the printed number field and type the printed number (e.g. 0473) or the full code (e.g. BAG-4237-03). Case-insensitive.
Expected: On a match, the app navigates to /depot/process/[bagId]. The header shows Aguirre's name, the printed number, and the bag's declared type ("Commingled" or "Separated · 2× multiplier").

Verify:

Scanning a random QR (not from this route) is rejected with a clear message

Tapping a bag row directly — without scanning — also opens the form

Canceling the scanner returns to the bag list without errors

Scenario 4 — Process a clean bag

Depot Signed in as Goode.

For this test, the "bag" can be seven little piles of paper on Goode's table. The goal is that the numbers she types turn into points for Aguirre.

  1. Leave Contamination on None (green).
  2. Type weights in each of the seven material rows. Suggested starter values:
    • 🥫 Aluminum — 1.4
    • 🧴 PET — 2.1
    • 📦 Cardboard — 0.6
    • Leave the other four at zero.
  3. Watch the Total · X lbs · +Y points card recompute as you type.
  4. Tap ✓ Submit & next bag.
Expected: A green "points awarded" flash, then Goode bounces back to the route bag list. The bag she just processed now reads PROCESSED. Sign in as Aguirre in another browser — her points balance has gone up by exactly the amount Goode saw on submit.

Verify:

The preview total matches what Aguirre sees after submit

Aguirre's latest transaction row (on her rewards / profile screen) shows type pickup with a positive delta

The bag's status on the route list flipped to Processed on reload

Optional

Advanced — edge cases

Skip this section unless you're debugging a specific behavior. The scenarios below cover contamination penalties, the 2× separated-bag multiplier, re-processing guards, SMS stub output, and the Inventory dashboard. None are required to sign off on Phase 7 — the happy path above is the release gate.

Scenario 5 — Contamination changes the math

Depot Signed in as Goode.

Open a different pending bag and type the same weights as Scenario 4. Before submitting, try each contamination level and watch the preview.

  1. With weights entered, tap None, Minor, Major, then Severe in turn.
  2. Note the preview point count at each.
  3. Finally leave it on Severe and submit.
Expected: The four previews are roughly 100%, 70%, 40%, and 10% of the "None" amount — because the penalties are 0 / 30 / 60 / 90%. The submitted severe bag awards Aguirre only ~10% of what the equivalent clean bag did.

Verify:

The four preview amounts step down correctly

Submitting a severe bag is allowed (it's not a block — just a big penalty)

The contaminationSeverity is preserved on the bagProcessing record (admin view, when Phase 9 lands)

Scenario 6 — Separated bags get the 2× multiplier

Admin + Resident + Depot All three.

This scenario needs a bag that Aguirre declared as separated. If the Phase 6 test data didn't cover that, set one up now:

  1. As Aguirre, scan or declare a new bag with the Separated option (this is the pre-pickup scan flow). If the UI doesn't expose the toggle, ask the dev team to set bags.declaredType = 'separated' on a test bag.
  2. As Aguirre, run that bag through a pickup on today's route.
  3. As Aguirre, close the route and deliver to depot.
  4. As Goode, open the bag in the process form.
Expected: The header on the process form reads "Declared · Separated (2× multiplier)". The live points preview is double what the same weights would be for a commingled bag at the same contamination level.

Verify:

Separated bags show the 2× label in the form header

Preview is roughly 2× the matching commingled bag

Scenario 7 — Re-processing is refused

Depot Signed in as Goode.

  1. From the route bag list, try to tap an already-PROCESSED row.
  2. Then open the scanner and try to scan that same processed bag.
Expected: The row is disabled (greyed out, no click). The scanner shows "Bag #<number> is already processed" and drops back to the list instead of opening the form.

Verify:

Processed rows can't be reopened

Scanning a processed QR flashes a message, not the form

Scenario 8 — Route progress fills in

Depot Signed in as Goode.

  1. Process a couple of bags from Aguirre's route but leave at least one still pending.
  2. Tap ← Back to return to Incoming.
Expected: The route's badge has moved from NEW to IN PROGRESS, a thin green bar shows how far along Goode is, and the subtitle reads "Processing X/Y". After the last bag is processed, the badge flips to DONE.

Verify:

Badge transitions NEW → IN PROGRESS → DONE based on bag status

Progress bar width matches the X/Y

Scenario 9 — Reading the Inventory dashboard

Depot Signed in as Goode.

  1. Tap Inventory in the top nav.
  2. Review the Current stock (lbs) card for each of the seven materials.
  3. Review the Yellow sheet (this month) card.
Expected:
  • The weights reflect exactly what Goode has submitted so far, summed per material (aluminum = 1.4 × number of clean bags with 1.4 lb of aluminum, etc.).
  • Each row has a progress bar that turns amber near 80% and red at 95% of the pilot capacity (1,500 lbs per material). If any material hits 95%, a red alert banner appears above the cards.
  • The Yellow Sheet lists each material's current market price per pound (not the customer share; the split stays admin-side).

Verify:

Stock numbers match Goode's submissions

Bars visibly change color at the amber and red thresholds

Yellow sheet values match what Tia set in /admin/pricing

Scenario 10 — SMS notification stub fires

Tester With access to the dev console.

Twilio isn't wired for the pilot, so Aguirre won't see a real text. But the app should still log the intent so the post-pilot Twilio swap has something to hook into.

  1. Watch the dev-server terminal while Goode submits a bag.
  2. After submit, check the smsLog collection (Firestore console or a dev query).
Expected: A console line like [sms-stub] to=... purpose=bag_processed body=Your bag #0473 was processed. +21,800 points added to your balance. plus a new smsLog document with the same purpose and a relatedDocId pointing at the bagProcessing record just created.

Verify:

One stub log per successful submit

Failed submits (e.g., network error) do not leave a stub log behind

Quick troubleshooting

What you see Likely cause What to try
"No depot is assigned to your account" Goode's invite didn't include a depot, or no depot exists yet. Have Tia re-invite Goode with the Depot dropdown set, or create a depot first in /admin/depots.
Incoming is empty but Aguirre finished a route Aguirre didn't tap Deliver to depot, or the route's zone isn't mapped to this depot. Ask Aguirre to re-open the app — the summary page should show an "All stops handled" card with the Deliver to depot button. Or have Tia check zone↔depot mapping in /admin/depots.
Camera stays black Browser blocked camera access (especially on desktop Safari). Reload and grant permission. Manual entry is always available.
Preview points stay at 0 All weight fields are empty or zero. Type a non-zero weight in any row — even 0.1 lb unlocks the form.
Submit button greyed out Total weight is 0, or the form is already submitting. Wait for the spinner, or add at least one weight.
Aguirre says she didn't get points Two possible causes: (1) Goode's submit threw an error (check for a red banner), (2) Aguirre is looking at a cached dashboard. Have Aguirre reload her rewards / profile page. If still missing, check Firestore for a transactions doc with type=pickup and her uid.

What's not testable yet

Depot manager screens (Phase 8) are where mill pickups subtract from inventory — until that ships, inventory only grows. Contamination strike tracking (Phase 9) will generate warning letters for repeat offenders based on the severity flags Goode sets here; for now the flags just sit on the bagProcessing records. Real Twilio SMS ships post-pilot. None of those gaps should block Phase 7 verification.
