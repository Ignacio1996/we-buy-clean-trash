Operator Testing as an operator

The driver's day. Start a route, scan bags at each stop, deliver sticker sheets, close out at the depot. ~25 minutes once the prereqs are in place.

Device: 📱 Phone (primary — the app is designed phone-first). 💻 Laptop on the side for Tia, if you need to set up or inspect routes.
Who you'll be: Operator Aguirre (operator.aguirre@wbct-pilot.test).

⚠️ What's stubbed in this flow
  • SMS to residents (driver-on-the-way, pickup complete) is logged to the server console, not sent as a real text.
  • "Driver on the way" is tap-triggered by you, not auto-fired from GPS geofencing.

Your testing path

The operator flow has prerequisites that must be done by an admin first. Don't skip step 0.

0

Prerequisites (admin must do these first)

Until Tia has built a route for you, the operator home screen just says "No active route." Ask her (or do it yourself on the admin laptop) to:

  • Set Yellow Sheet prices — Phase 4 · Scenario 5.
  • Make sure a resident has a scanned bag ready for pickup — Phase 3 · Scenario 4.
  • Build today's route and assign it to you — Phase 5.
1

Sign in on a phone

Sign in as the operator. You should land at /operator and see today's route.

→ Accounts & sign-in · Scenario 5 (or just use the seeded operator)

2

See today's route, start it, text "on the way"

Confirm you see the correct stops, no one else's. Start the route, then tap the driver-on-the-way button (SMS is stubbed — button just shows a toast).

→ Operator day · Scenarios 1–2

3

Complete a pickup (scan a QR, take a photo)

Scan the resident's bag sticker. If the camera misbehaves, use the manual code entry fallback. Take any photo for the "I was here" record.

→ Operator day · Scenario 3

4

Try Skip vs Not Out vs Issue

Three ways a stop can end badly — confirm each has the expected follow-up (skip = retry later, not-out = marked for next visit, issue = logged for admin).

→ Operator day · Scenario 4

5

Deliver a new sticker sheet

Hand off a sheet to a resident with a pending bag order. Claims 10 bags to them atomically.

→ Operator day · Scenario 5

6

Close out the route

Tap "Deliver to depot" on the summary screen. Once you do, the route shows up under the depot worker's Incoming list — handoff complete.

→ Operator day · Scenario 6

7

Manual scan when routing fails

Tap "Scan a bag manually" on your home screen — it's always there, even on a day with no route assigned. Scan any resident's bag sticker (or type its printed number) to log the pickup without needing it to be on your route. It's the safety net for when the routing system is down or a bag was never scheduled.

You'll see whose bag it is before you confirm. If the resident never scheduled it, the app marks it ad-hoc and asks you to note whether it's separated or mixed. Then it's the same condition + photo steps as a normal stop, and the bag still credits the resident at the depot weigh-in. A bag already picked up or processed is refused, so you can't double-collect.

When you're done. Tick every checkbox in the Phase 6 guide and report any regressions. The depot worker takes over from here.

What you're not testing

  • GPS geofencing — driver-on-the-way is a tap, not automatic.
  • Real SMS to residents — all stubbed.
