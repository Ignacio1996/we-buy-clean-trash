Compost · Admin — Commercial accounts

Onboarding compost-collection sites and provisioning their reusable bins. Replaces Tia's Linktree + Google Sheet "Directory" tab. Run as Tia Johnson.

⚠️ Where this fits Phase B of the compost integration. Sites onboarded here become the operator's compost route in Compost · Operator route. City-format diversion reports across these sites are now live — see Compost · Diversion reports (Phase C).

What you're verifying

  • Tia can add a commercial site (business name, contact, address, schedule, materials).
  • The site picks a default bin size and a list of pickup days.
  • An affiliation tag (Compost Clubhouse vs City of Columbus) can be set free-form.
  • Tia can provision N bins for the site — each gets a BIN-NNNN QR code that the driver scans on-site.
  • Tia can print QR labels for a site's bins (Rollo 2.25×1.25 or a 4×6 sheet) to stick on the physical bins.
  • Archiving a site preserves its bins and historical pickups.
Scenario 1 — Add a Compost Clubhouse site

Admin Signed in as Tia Johnson.

  1. From the side nav, open 🏢 Commercial sites. (Sits between Bag stickers and Pricing.)
  2. Click + Add commercial site.
  3. Fill out:
    • Business name: Comune
    • Contact name: Joe Galarraga
    • Contact phone / email: optional
    • Street / unit / city / state / ZIP
    • Zone: the zone you set up in the admin Zones page
    • Default bin: 32 Gallon
    • Pickups / week: 2
    • Bins on site: 3 — the declared count of bins at the location. Shows in the "Bins on Site" column of the diversion report. (Metadata only; it doesn't have to match the number of QR-coded bins you provision below.)
    • First month of data: optional YYYY-MM — the month this site started collecting. Used as the site's report start so "months active" is counted from the right point. Tia's imported sites already have this set from her spreadsheet.
    • Affiliation tag: compost_clubhouse
    • Collection days: click Mon and Thu
    • Materials: Food scrap should be pre-selected (it's the only bin-fullness material seeded by Phase A). Leave it on.
    • Driver notes: "Bin is in the alley behind the kitchen — gate code 4321."
  4. Click Create site.
Expected: The form closes and a card for Comune appears under Active sites. Bins counter shows 0. The amber affiliation chip reads compost_clubhouse.
Scenario 2 — Provision 3 bins for the site
  1. On the Comune card, click Provision bins (top-right of the bin section).
  2. Pick size 32 Gallon, count 3.
  3. Click Add bins.
Expected: The counter jumps from 0 to 3 bins provisioned. Three new BIN-NNNN-coded BagDocs now exist in Firestore, each tagged reusable: true and commercialAccountId pointing at Comune. Print their QR labels next (Scenario 3).

Verify:

Bins counter on the site card increments to 3

Trying count: 100 is rejected (cap is 50/req)

Scenario 3 — Print QR labels for the bins

Each provisioned bin already has a unique BIN-NNNN code — this step renders those codes as printable QR labels to stick on the physical bins. The driver then scans the label at pickup (see Compost · Operator route).

  1. On the Comune card's bin section, click Print QR labels (appears next to Provision bins once at least one bin exists). It opens a print page in a new tab.
  2. Pick a label size with the toggle in the top bar:
    • 2.25 × 1.25 — Rollo thermal stock, one label per page.
    • 4 × 6 — up to 10 labels on a single sheet (2 columns × 5 rows), paginating automatically for sites with more bins.
  3. Each label shows the QR code, the site name, the BIN-NNNN number, and the bin size (e.g. "32 Gallon compost bin").
  4. Click Print and send to your label printer. Stick one label on each bin.
Expected: The preview shows one label per provisioned bin. The QR encodes the bin's BIN-NNNN code, so scanning it at a stop matches the right bin row. Long business names wrap to two lines rather than getting cut off.

Verify:

"Print QR labels" only appears when the site has ≥ 1 bin

Switching 2.25×1.25 ↔ 4×6 re-lays out the labels

The printed number under the QR matches the bin's code (human-readable fallback)

Scenario 4 — Edit and archive

Archiving must not destroy historical pickups or detach the bins.

  1. On any active site card, click Archive.
  2. Confirm.
Expected: The site moves into the Archived section at the bottom. Its bins still exist in Firestore (verify by querying bags where commercialAccountId == <archived-id>). Past binPickups docs continue to reference the site by id.

Edge cases worth poking

  • Zone required: The "+ Add commercial site" button is disabled until at least one zone exists.
  • Material picker: Only materials with measurementMode: 'bin_fullness' default-on. Cash recyclables (aluminum, etc.) can be selected too — it just means the operator could record those at this site if you ever want to.
  • Collection days mismatch: The form lets you pick fewer days than pickupsPerWeek says. That's intentional — drivers might double up on a single day.
