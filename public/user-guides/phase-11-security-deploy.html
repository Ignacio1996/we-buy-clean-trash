Phase 11 · Post-deploy smoke test

After the dev team pushes the production build, do this 5-minute check to confirm nothing obvious broke. No new features — you're just proving every role can still sign in and open the pages they use every day.

Before you start. The dev team will tell you "the production deploy is live — please smoke-test." They'll give you the production URL. Use that URL for every step below. If you can't sign in at all on the production URL, stop and tell the dev team — don't continue.

The smoke test (≈5 minutes)

Sign in as each role, open the pages listed, and confirm they load without a red error banner. Tick the box when a role passes. If anything fails, note which role and which page and send it back to the dev team.

If something doesn't work

What you see What to do
A page loads but shows a red banner mentioning permission or access. Screenshot the page + URL and send to the dev team. Don't try to fix it.
A page shows a spinner forever, or a blank screen. Wait 30 seconds, then reload once. If it still doesn't load, screenshot + report.
You can't sign in at all on the production URL. Stop the smoke test. Tell the dev team — they probably missed an env var on Vercel.
A page that worked 5 minutes ago now throws an error. Could be a still-building search index. Wait 2–3 minutes and retry. If it keeps failing, report.
What "passes" means. You're not testing features here — just that the lights are on. If all four role cards above have a ticked checkbox and you didn't see any red banners, reply to the dev team with "smoke test passed" and the time you ran it. That's the whole handoff.
