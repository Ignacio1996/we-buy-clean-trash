Resident Testing as a resident

The homeowner path. Sign up, order bags, scan one before pickup, check points, try redeeming. ~20 minutes end-to-end with the seeded accounts.

Device: 📱 Phone (primary). 💻 Laptop works too but the resident app is designed phone-first.
Who you'll be: Casey Pearl (resident1.test@webuyclean.trash) from the Test users card.

Your testing path

Work through the steps below in order. Each step links to the relevant phase guide — treat this page as the map; the phase guides are the detail.

1

Sign in (or sign up from scratch)

Make sure sign-in works for your resident account. If you want to also experience the signup flow, create a new resident on top of the seeded ones.

→ Accounts & sign-in · Scenarios 1–3

2

Explore the dashboard

Confirm the points balance, next-pickup card, and bottom nav all load. This is the first screen a real pilot customer will see every day.

→ Resident app · Scenario 2

3

Order a sheet of bags

Place an order. The checkout is mocked — no real card is charged. Confirm the shipping rule toggles at $20 and the order shows up in your history.

→ Resident app · Scenario 3

4

Scan a bag before pickup day

Scan one of your sticker QR codes to register the bag as out for pickup. Ask the dev team to pre-issue a sheet if you don't have one yet.

→ Resident app · Scenario 4

5

Try the pre-signup AI scan

Open an incognito window and tap Try the scan on the landing page. Point the camera at a recyclable. Confirm you get an estimate without needing to sign up.

→ Resident app · Scenario 1

6

Check the payout calculator + rewards

Play with the calculator to see how different weights translate to points, then open Rewards to see your signup bonus ledger entry.

→ Resident app · Scenarios 5–7

When you're done. Tick every checkbox in the Phase 3 guide, note anything that didn't look right, and send the list back. You don't need to touch any other phase as a resident.

What you're not testing

  • "Pay trash bill" and "Donate" redemptions — disabled on purpose during the pilot.
  • Real gift-card fulfillment — the request queues for an admin to send manually.
  • Real SMS alerts — all messages log to the server console instead.
