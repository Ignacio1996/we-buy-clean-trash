Phase 3 · Resident App

Everything a homeowner can do: estimate their trash value before signing up, order recycling bags, scan a bag before pickup, check their points, and redeem a gift card. All scenarios in this guide are run as Resident Aguirre unless noted.

⚠️ What's stubbed in this flow Some features look real but don't fully fire during the pilot. That's by design — please don't file these as bugs:
  • Ordering bags — checkout runs through Stripe's hosted test checkout. Use Stripe test cards only; no live card is charged during the pilot.
  • Redeeming a gift card — the request lands in an admin queue; the actual gift card is emailed manually by an admin, not auto-fulfilled. Pilot-gated: each resident can redeem once, and Redeem buttons lock once the pilot cap is reached.
  • "Pay trash bill" and "Donate" buttons are disabled on purpose.
  • SMS notifications are logged to the server console, not sent by text during the pilot.

What you're verifying

  • A visitor can try the AI scan before signing up.
  • A signed-in resident's dashboard shows the right numbers (points, next pickup).
  • Ordering bags produces an order confirmation.
  • Scanning a bag before pickup day registers it as "out for pickup."
  • The payout calculator reflects current material pricing.
  • Gift card redemptions show up in the admin queue (cross-checked in the Admin guide).
Scenario 1 — Pre-signup AI scan (visitor, no account yet)

Visitor No one signed in.

  1. Open the homepage in a private window on your phone.
  2. Tap Try the scan (or the big camera button on the landing page).
  3. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
  4. Point the camera at a small pile of recyclables (a soda can, a cardboard box, a plastic bottle) and tap the shutter.
  5. Wait a few seconds.
Expected: A list of detected items appears, each with an estimated weight and an estimated dollar value. A total at the bottom shows what the pile would earn. A Sign up to claim button is below the results.

Verify:

Camera opens and captures an image

AI returns a list of items (not an error)

Closing the tab and reopening shows no saved scan (it's meant to be session-only)

The estimate won't be perfect. The AI is rough on purpose — its job is to show the resident that their trash has real value, not to be accurate to the cent. If it labels a can as a bottle, note it, but don't call it a bug.
Scenario 1b — Signing up (NEW · Jun 9)

Visitor No account yet.

  1. From the landing page, tap Sign up to claim (or Sign up).
  2. On the Four simple things step, read the neo-brutalist sticker cards (white cards with ink borders, numbered badges, and mint/peach chips highlighting the free-delivery perk).
  3. On the address step, start typing your street address. Google Places Autocomplete suggests addresses and auto-fills street, city, state, and ZIP when you pick one. Unit/apt is still typed in by hand.
  4. Confirm that once a ZIP is filled in, the page shows the matched pickup day(s) for that zone (a zone can have more than one pickup day per week).
  5. Finish signup and land on the resident home.
Expected: Address autocomplete fills the fields cleanly, the matched pickup day(s) appear after the ZIP is entered, and the new account receives a welcome email shortly after creation.

Verify:

Address autocomplete suggests and auto-fills street/city/state/zip (unit/apt stays manual)

Matched pickup day(s) show after the ZIP is entered — more than one day if the zone has multiple

"Four simple things" renders as sticker cards with numbered badges and the free-delivery chip

A welcome email arrives at the signup address

Resident Signed in as Aguirre.

You should see:

Scenario 3 — Ordering bags

Resident Signed in as Aguirre.

  1. Tap Order more bags on the home screen.
  2. Use the + / – stepper to choose a quantity. Try 1 first.
  3. Read the order summary. With 1 box, you should see a $10 delivery line because the order is under $20.
  4. Bump the quantity to 3. Delivery should drop to Free once the subtotal clears $20.
  5. Tap Pay now — checkout runs through Stripe's hosted page.
Stripe checkout (NEW · Jun 9)

Pay now hands off to Stripe's hosted checkout. Orders whose welcome credit fully covers the cart ($0 due) skip Stripe entirely and go straight to the success page.

Expected: After paying on Stripe (or instantly, for a $0 welcome-credit order), you land on an order confirmation / success screen with an order number and a "delivered on next pickup" line. The order appears in Aguirre's account history.

Verify:

Delivery rule toggles at the $20 threshold

"Pay now" opens Stripe's hosted checkout

NEW: A cart fully covered by welcome credit ($0 due) skips Stripe and lands on the success page

Order appears in Aguirre's order history after checkout

Use Stripe test cards only during the pilot. Checkout is Stripe's real hosted page running in test mode. Don't enter a live card number — use a Stripe test card (e.g. 4242 4242 4242 4242). If you're ever unsure whether you're on test mode, stop and report it.
Scenario 3b — Viewing order history

Resident Signed in as Aguirre.

Once an order has been placed, Aguirre should be able to see it in her history — including delivered and cancelled orders that no longer show on the home dashboard.

  1. From the home screen, tap View all → on the pending-orders card. (If there are no pending orders, use the View past orders → link in the empty-state card instead.)
  2. You should land on /resident/order-bags/history with a list of every bag order, newest first.
  3. Go back to Order bags and tap Past orders → in the top-right of that screen — it should take you to the same history page.
  4. If Aguirre has no orders yet, the page should show an empty state with an Order bags → call-to-action.
Expected: Each row shows the sheet/bag count, total charged, current status (Delivered / Queued / Out for delivery / Cancelled), the placed date, and either the delivered date (if delivered) or the upcoming route date.

Verify:

Home dashboard → View all link opens the history page

Order Bags screen → Past orders link opens the history page

Orders are sorted newest-first

Delivered orders show a delivered date; pending/queued orders show an ETA or Delivery date TBD

A resident with no orders sees the empty state (not a blank list)

Scenario 4 — Scanning a bag before pickup day

Resident Signed in as Aguirre on her phone.

Prerequisite: Aguirre needs at least one unused bag sticker in her account. The dev team can pre-issue a sheet to her for testing, or Aguirre the operator will deliver one during the operator guide.

  1. Tap Scan on the bottom nav.
  2. Allow camera access.
  3. Hold the camera over the QR code on one of her stickers.
  4. If the camera can't read the sticker, tap Enter code manually and type the printed number (something like BAG-4237-03).
  5. Pick Separated or Mixed for what's inside the bag.
  6. Take the "before" photo of the filled bag on her doorstep.
  7. Submit.
Expected: Aguirre sees a "Your bag is scheduled for pickup" confirmation and the bag now shows up in the Upcoming pickups area of her dashboard.

Verify:

QR scanner reads the sticker directly

Manual code entry works as a fallback

Picking "Separated" vs "Mixed" is saved (visible in the pickup details)

Scenario 5 — Payout calculator

Resident Signed in as Aguirre.

  1. Tap Calculator on the bottom nav.
  2. Type weights into each material field (try 1 lb aluminum, 2 lb cardboard, etc.). Each input now shows a lbs suffix on the right (Apr 27 update).
  3. Toggle Clean & separated on and off.
  4. If admin has added a custom material (Phase 4 · Scenario 5b), confirm it appears in the input list with its custom name.
  5. If admin has scheduled a pricing campaign (Phase 4 · Scenario 5c), confirm a 🔥 Bonus running now banner appears at the top of the page, and the affected material's row in Accepted materials shows a ×N tag with the boosted "you get" amount.
Expected: The estimated points update live. Flipping the "separated" toggle roughly doubles the estimate. Dollar equivalents ($10 per 1,000 pts) match the point totals. With a ×2 campaign on aluminum, the same 1 lb of aluminum should award double the points it did before.

Verify:

Live updates as you type

Separated multiplier behaves correctly

NEW: Each input row shows the lbs suffix

NEW: Custom materials added by admin appear here within seconds (no redeploy required)

NEW: Live campaign multiplier compounds with the separated 2× — a 1 lb aluminum bag with separated + ×2 campaign earns ~4× the no-campaign mixed amount

Scenario 7 — Rewards and redemption

Resident Signed in as Aguirre.

  1. Tap Rewards on the bottom nav.
  2. Confirm the current balance matches the home screen.
  3. Look at the 4 redemption tiles: Amazon gift card, Walmart gift card, Pay trash bill, Donate to nonprofit.
  4. Tap Amazon gift card.
  5. Pick a dollar value (e.g. $5 = 500 pts).
  6. Submit the request.
Expected: Aguirre's points balance drops by the selected amount and a new entry appears in her history ("Amazon gift card — pending"). The request will also show up in Tia's admin redemption queue — you'll verify that in the Admin guide.

Pay trash bill and Donate tiles should be visible but disabled (greyed out) with a "Coming soon" label. That's intentional for the pilot.
Redemption is pilot-gated (NEW · Jun 9)

Each resident can redeem once during the pilot, and the Redeem buttons lock with a friendly notice once the overall pilot cap is reached. A locked button is expected behavior, not a bug.

Verify:

Balance decreases immediately

History shows the pending redemption

Pay trash bill / Donate are disabled, not crash buttons

NEW: After redeeming once (or when the pilot cap is hit), Redeem buttons lock with a friendly notice

When to stop and report

If any of these fail, note which scenario, what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened. Screenshots help. You don't need to re-test all of Phase 3 after a fix — just re-run the single scenario that failed.
