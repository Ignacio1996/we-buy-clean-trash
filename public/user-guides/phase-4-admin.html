Phase 4 · Admin Dashboard

Tia's back office: invites, zones, depots, material pricing, and the gift card queue. All scenarios in this guide are run as Tia Johnson.

⚠️ What's stubbed in this flow A few admin actions only simulate their external effect during the pilot. Treat these as expected, not bugs:
  • Invitation emails — generated and written to Firestore, but the email itself is logged to the server console (no real inbox delivery yet).
  • Gift card redemptions — approving a request does not auto-send a gift card; an admin emails it manually after approval.
  • Material price changes are real and do affect live resident calculations — be deliberate when you edit prices during testing.

What you're verifying

  • The overview dashboard shows real numbers, not placeholders.
  • Tia can send an operator invitation and it arrives (or simulates arriving).
  • Zones and depots can be created, edited, and deleted.
  • Material prices save and the resident calculator immediately reflects the change.
  • Gift card requests from residents appear in the redemption queue.

Scenario 4b — Per-depot accepted materials NEW · Apr 27

Admin Signed in as Tia Johnson.

Each depot can be configured to accept only a subset of materials (Kirk's example: a Tocco Hills depot that doesn't take HDPE or mixed plastic).

  2. Below the address, locate the Accepted materials (X/7) chip row.

  5. Now sign in as Depot Goode in another browser and open a pending bag in /depot/process.

  8. Back as Tia, re-enable HDPE, save, and confirm Goode sees the row again.
Expected: Depots that pre-date this feature (no acceptedMaterials field) behave as if they accept everything — that's the legacy fallback. The depot Inventory page also keeps showing materials that have been processed historically even if they were later removed from the accepted set.

Verify:

Saving requires at least one material — empty selection is blocked

Toggling a chip without saving doesn't persist (Reset link clears edits)

Scenario 5b — Add a custom material (Compost / Textile / Electronics) NEW · Apr 27

Admin Signed in as Tia Johnson.

The seed yellow sheet ships with 7 materials. Admins can extend it on the fly when a new commodity comes online (e.g. compost), or hide an existing one when its market crashes. New materials are soft-deleted only — historical records stay intact.

  1. Open Pricing. Scroll to the dashed + Add to yellow sheet composer below the table.
  2. Type Compost for the name, leave $/lb at 0.00, payout at 0.
  3. Click + Add to yellow sheet. A new row appears with id compost and a green new badge.
  4. Click Save pricing. The badge clears; a priceHistory snapshot is written.
  5. Now uncheck the Active box on the Mixed Plastic row to simulate a market crash. Save.
Expected: Compost shows up on the resident calculator and on Goode's process form (assuming the depot has it in its accepted set — see Scenario 4b). Mixed Plastic disappears from both the calculator and the process form. Historical mixed-plastic inventory and bagProcessing rows are still readable in admin reports.

Verify:

Names slugify correctly: "Mixed Glass" → mixed_glass, "E-Waste" → e_waste

Trying to add a duplicate id is blocked client-side

Scenario 5c — Pricing campaign (×2 on Aluminum) NEW · Apr 27

Admin Signed in as Tia Johnson.

From the meeting: "if we know the price of aluminum is real high, can we send out alerts like, aluminum is three times the points today?" — yes. Pricing Campaigns apply a global ×N multiplier to selected materials over a date range. Multipliers compound when multiple campaigns overlap on the same material.

  1. On the Pricing page, scroll past the yellow sheet to the Pricing campaigns section.
  2. Name: Aluminum 2× week. Multiplier: 2. Starts: today. Ends: 7 days out.
  3. Toggle the Aluminum material chip on. Click Start campaign.
  4. The new campaign appears in Active & upcoming with a LIVE badge.
  5. In another browser, sign in as Aguirre. The dashboard now shows a 🔥 BONUS RUNNING NOW banner; the calculator's Aluminum row has a ×2 tag.
  6. Sign in as Goode and open a pending bag — a 🔥 Active campaign banner appears above contamination, and the points preview applies the multiplier.
  7. Submit a bag with 1 lb aluminum. Compare the awarded points against a control bag with no campaign — should be ~2× higher.
  8. Click End now on the campaign row. Confirm the banners disappear in both browsers within seconds.
Expected: Multiplier compounds correctly: schedule a ×1.5 on aluminum on top of the live ×2 — preview should reflect ×3. The applied campaign array is snapshotted onto the bagProcessing doc (appliedCampaigns field) so historical points are auditable even after the campaign ends.

Verify:

"End now" sets active: false and pulls endsAt to now

Scheduling a campaign with end ≤ start is blocked

Campaign with no materials selected is blocked

Resident dashboard banner only shows live campaigns (not future ones)
