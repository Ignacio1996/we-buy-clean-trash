We Buy Clean Trash

The full lifecycle — from doorstep to recycling mill

🛒

Buy the Bag

Customer orders special recycling bags online. Each bag has a unique QR code that tracks it through the entire system.

💰 Revenue: bag sales
1
2
♻️

Fill & Separate

Two bag types: Cardboard & Paper in one bag, Commingled (glass, plastic, aluminum cans, tin) in another. Separated earns double points; commingled earns base points.

📱

Scan & Schedule

Customer scans the QR code in the app, selects bag type (cardboard/paper or commingled), takes a photo, and places it at the door. The app confirms the next scheduled pickup date and time.

3
4
🚐

Operator Picks Up

A driver in a small van arrives and scans the QR code again. The system now knows: this bag left this address at this time.

🏭

Depot: Sort & Weigh

At the depot, bags are scanned again, opened, and checked for contamination. For commingled bags, only total weight is recorded. For separated bags, material-specific weight is logged. Results are sent back to the system.

5
6
⭐

Points Awarded

Based on material type and weight, the customer earns points. They receive an email notification with their points earned and environmental impact — trees saved, water preserved, carbon footprint reduced.

🎁

Redeem Rewards

Cash out points for money, pay their trash bill, or donate to a nonprofit. Future: partner store bonuses (Walmart, Home Depot, etc.).

7
8
📦

Sell to Mills

Sorted materials are palletized and sold to domestic and international recycling mills at market rates (the "yellow sheet").

💰 Revenue: 70% margin on sales

Who Uses the App

🏠

Resident

Orders bags, fills them, scans QR codes, earns & redeems points

🚐

Operator

Picks up bags, scans at doorstep, delivers to depot

⚖️

Depot Worker

Scans bags, checks for contamination, weighs materials, enters data into system

🏢

Depot Manager

Monitors depot inventory, manages multiple depot locations, schedules pickups from mills

📊

Admin

Master dashboard — sees all data, all districts, all revenue

Points System

Signup bonus: 10,000 pts  ·  ~500 pts per bag  ·  Separated = double points  ·  Commingled = base points  ·  Contamination = lost points

Redeem at 100,000 pts  ·  Bonus points and multiplier weeks to build the habit early
